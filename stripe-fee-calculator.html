Stripe fee FAQs

How much does Stripe take per transaction?
2.9% + $0.30 for a standard US online card payment. On a $100 sale that's $3.20 in fees and $96.80 in your pocket. There are no monthly or setup fees on the standard plan — you only pay per transaction.
What about international customers?
Stripe adds a 1.5% cross-border surcharge when the card was issued outside the US, making it 4.4% + $0.30. If currency conversion is also needed, add another 1% — up to 5.4% + $0.30 total. International sales cost nearly double the domestic rate.
Is ACH cheaper than cards on Stripe?
Much cheaper for larger amounts: 0.8% capped at $5. A $1,000 card payment costs $29.30 in fees; via ACH it's just $5. For B2B invoices and large transfers, ACH is the clear winner.
Does Stripe refund fees when I refund a customer?
No — this is the painful one. Stripe charges no extra fee to issue a refund, but the original processing fee is not returned. Refunding a $100 sale costs you the $3.20 fee permanently.
Stripe vs PayPal — which is cheaper?
For website checkouts Stripe wins: 2.9% + $0.30 vs PayPal Checkout's 3.49% + $0.49. On a $100 online sale, Stripe costs $3.20 while PayPal Checkout costs $3.98 — a $0.78 difference that adds up fast at volume. Try our PayPal fee calculator to compare.
