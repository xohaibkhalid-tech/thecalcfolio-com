Depop vs Poshmark Fees: Which Keeps You More?

Calculate it for your own numbersFree, no sign-up — every fee on one receipt.
Depop Fee Calculator →

The short answer: Depop keeps you more — it's not close. Depop charges 0% commission (just 3.3% + $0.45 processing). Poshmark takes a flat 20% on sales of $15+. On a $50 sale, that's $47.90 vs $40.00 — nearly $8 difference per sale.

But fees aren't the whole story. Here's the full comparison, including the one thing Poshmark does better.

The twist: Poshmark's one advantage

Depop's buyer fee (up to 5% + $1 at checkout) is the catch. Your $50 listing costs the buyer up to $53.50 on Depop — on Poshmark it's exactly $50. For price-sensitive buyers, that checkout surprise can kill a sale before it happens. Poshmark's simplicity converts; Depop's fees reward you after the sale.

The smart play: list on both. Price slightly lower on Depop to offset the buyer fee, and let the 0% commission do the rest. Many resellers run the same inventory on both platforms and let the math decide.

Quick answers

Is Depop really free to sell on?

Commission-free, yes — for US/UK sellers. You still pay 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing per sale, and buyers pay a marketplace fee at checkout. "Free" means no commission, not zero costs.

Why does anyone still use Poshmark at 20%?

Audience and ease. Poshmark's buyer pool is huge, the flat 20% is dead simple to calculate, and there's no buyer-side fee surprising shoppers at checkout. For some sellers, more sales at 80% beats fewer sales at 96%.

Which is better for cheap items under $15?

Depop. Poshmark's $2.95 flat fee on sub-$15 sales is brutal — on a $10 sale that's 29.5%. Depop's 3.3% + $0.45 is $0.78 on the same sale.

Fee rates: Depop and Poshmark US, verified October 2026. Platforms change fees periodically — verify current rates before pricing.
