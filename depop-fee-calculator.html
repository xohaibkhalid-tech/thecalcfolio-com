Depop fees — quick answers

Does Depop really charge 0% commission now?

Yes — for US and UK sellers, Depop removed its old 10% selling fee. You now pay only the 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing fee. The catch: buyers pay a marketplace fee (up to 5% + $1) at checkout, which can make your items look pricier to them.

Are boosted listings worth the 8%?

Only if the math works. The 8% applies only when the boost directly leads to the sale — but on a $50 item that's $4 extra, nearly tripling your fees. Test it on slow-moving inventory, not everything.

Depop vs Poshmark — which keeps me more?

Depop, by a mile. Poshmark takes 20% on sales over $15; Depop takes 0% commission + 3.3% + $0.45 processing. On a $50 sale: Depop keeps you ~$47.90, Poshmark ~$40.

Do I pay fees on shipping?

Depop calculates the processing fee on the full order total (item + shipping + tax), so yes — the 3.3% applies to shipping too. Price accordingly.

What about the buyer fee — does it hurt my sales?

It can. Buyers see up to 5% + $1 added at checkout, which may make them hesitate on price-sensitive items. Competitive pricing matters more than ever on Depop.
