Venmo fees — quick answers

Is receiving money on Venmo free?

For personal payments from friends — yes, completely free. The fees kick in when money changes hands for business: 1.9% + $0.10 on a business profile, or 2.99% on personal-account payments tagged as Goods & Services.

Business profile or Goods & Services — which is cheaper?

The business profile (1.9% + $0.10) beats Goods & Services (2.99%) on every amount. On a $100 sale that's $2.00 vs $2.99. If you sell regularly, the business profile pays for itself instantly — it's free to set up.

How do I avoid the instant transfer fee?

Use the standard transfer (1–3 business days) — it's free. The 1.75% instant fee only buys you speed. Unless you need the money today, patience saves you $1.75 per $100.

Does Venmo charge the sender or the receiver?

Depends: credit-card funding fees (3%) are paid by the sender. Business and Goods & Services fees come out of the receiver's amount. Instant transfer fees are paid by whoever cashes out.

Can I use Venmo for international payments?

No — Venmo accounts are US-only. For international transfers, look at PayPal or a dedicated transfer service instead.
