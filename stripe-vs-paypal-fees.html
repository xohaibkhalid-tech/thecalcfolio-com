Stripe vs PayPal Fees: The Real Cost Compared

Calculate it for your own numbersFree, no sign-up — every fee on one receipt.
Stripe Fee Calculator →PayPal Fee Calculator →

The short answer: on price, Stripe wins every time online — 2.9% + $0.30 against PayPal Checkout's 3.49% + $0.49. On a $100 sale that's $3.20 vs $3.98. PayPal's case isn't price; it's the hundreds of millions of buyers who already have an account and check out in two clicks, plus a micropayments rate Stripe doesn't offer at all.

This is the expanded comparison. Every dollar figure below is worked out from the published rates — no rounded guesses, no "about" numbers.

Two PayPal rates trip people up, so get them straight: 3.49% + $0.49 is PayPal Checkout — the wallet button your buyer clicks. 2.99% + $0.49 is when a buyer pays by card directly through PayPal without using a wallet. Same checkout page, different rate, and the difference is $0.50 on every $100 sale.

Small sales: where PayPal actually wins

The fixed fee hurts most on tiny transactions — and this is the one place PayPal has a card Stripe can't play. PayPal's opt-in micropayments rate is 4.99% + $0.09: a higher percentage, but a much smaller fixed fee.

The verdict, by business type

  • Online store: Stripe as your default card processor. Cheaper on every sale, and its checkout and fraud tools are built for stores. Offer PayPal as an express option alongside it — many stores find the conversion lift covers the higher PayPal fee.
  • SaaS / subscriptions: Stripe, no contest. Billing, dunning, trials, and prorations are what Stripe was built for; PayPal's subscription tooling is thinner.
  • Marketplace seller: follow your buyers. If your customers expect PayPal (eBay-style buyers, older demographics), dropping it to save $0.78 a sale is penny-wise. If your marketplace runs on Stripe Connect, that's your answer.
  • In person: PayPal, on rate alone — 2.29% + $0.09 beats Stripe's 2.7% + $0.05. On a $100 sale that's $2.38 vs $2.75. (If you're choosing a counter setup from scratch, also compare Square — but between these two, PayPal's in-person rate wins.)
  • Micropayments (under $10): PayPal micropayments. At $3 it's $0.24 against Stripe's $0.39, and it stays ahead up to about $10. Apply for the rate; it isn't the default.
  • International sales: Stripe. The cross-border gap is $0.78 on a $100 domestic-priced sale and widens to $3.78 once currency conversion is involved.

The setup most growing stores land on: Stripe for cards, PayPal as the express button next to it. You pay the higher PayPal fee only on the sales buyers choose to route through PayPal — and those are often sales you'd have lost without it.

Quick answers

Is Stripe cheaper than PayPal?

Online, yes — at every amount. Stripe is 2.9% + $0.30; PayPal card payments are 2.99% + $0.49 and PayPal Checkout is 3.49% + $0.49. On a $100 sale that's $3.20 vs $3.48 vs $3.98. In person it's the other way round: PayPal's 2.29% + $0.09 beats Stripe's 2.7% + $0.05.

What is PayPal's fee on $100?

$3.98 if the buyer pays through PayPal Checkout ($100 × 3.49% + $0.49), or $3.48 if they pay by card directly through PayPal ($100 × 2.99% + $0.49). The two are often confused — check which flow your buyer used before comparing.

Why is PayPal Checkout 3.49% but card payments 2.99%?

PayPal charges more for wallet payments (Checkout, Guest Checkout, Venmo) than for a straight card payment processed through its system. Person-to-person Goods & Services payments are a separate 2.99% with no fixed fee — that's not the merchant Checkout rate.

Does PayPal have a micropayments rate?

Yes — 4.99% + $0.09 per transaction, for lower-value sales. You have to apply for it. It beats Stripe on sales under about $10 and beats standard PayPal Checkout up to about $27.

Which is better for international sales?

Stripe. Its international rate is 4.4% + $0.30 with a 1% conversion fee; PayPal Checkout runs 4.99% + $0.49 with a 3–4% conversion spread. On a $100 cross-border sale with conversion, that's $5.70 vs $9.48.

Can I use both Stripe and PayPal?

Yes — most platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce) support Stripe for cards plus PayPal Express side by side. It's the most common setup for growing stores: Stripe default, PayPal as the express option.

What does a chargeback cost on each?

Stripe charges a $15 dispute fee when one is received, plus $15 if you respond to it — the response fee comes back if you win. PayPal charges a $20 chargeback fee, plus a $15 standard dispute fee ($30 if you're a high-volume disputer).
