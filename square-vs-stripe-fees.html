Square vs Stripe Fees: Which Is Actually Cheaper?

Calculate it for your own numbersFree, no sign-up — every fee on one receipt.
Square Fee Calculator →Stripe Fee Calculator →

The short answer: online, Stripe wins every time (2.9% vs 3.3%). In person, it's a split decision with an exact tie at $100 — below that Stripe is cheaper, above it Square is. The rest is about your business, not the rates.

Most comparisons give you a vague "it depends." Here's the actual math, so you can stop wondering.

The rates, side by side (2026)

Note: Square overhauled pricing in October 2025 — its online rate jumped from 2.9% to 3.3% on the Free plan. Many old guides still show the pre-2025 numbers.

Online: Stripe wins, no contest

Same $0.30 fixed fee, but Stripe's 2.9% beats Square's 3.3% at every amount. On a $100 online sale: Stripe takes $3.20, Square takes $3.60. That's $0.40 per sale — $400 per 1,000 sales — for literally the same transaction.

If you sell mostly online, this article is over. Pick Stripe. The rest below is for in-person sellers.

In person: the $100 tie point

Here's the interesting bit. Square has the lower percentage (2.6% vs 2.7%) but the higher fixed fee ($0.15 vs $0.05). Those two differences cancel out at exactly $100:

So: coffee shop with $8 average tickets → Stripe. Furniture store with $600 tickets → Square. Right around $100 → flip a coin on fees and decide on features.

Beyond the rates

  • Hardware: Square's free card reader and POS ecosystem is the reason most in-person sellers choose it — Stripe's Terminal hardware costs more upfront.
  • Online tools: Stripe's API and checkout are the developer standard. If you need subscriptions, complex billing, or global payments, Stripe's toolkit is deeper.
  • Simplicity: Square is the easier all-in-one for non-technical sellers — POS, inventory, and payments in one account.
  • Monthly plans: Square's Plus plan ($49/mo) drops online to 2.9% + $0.30 — matching Stripe — but only makes sense above a certain volume.

Quick answers

Is Square cheaper than Stripe?

Online: no, Stripe is cheaper at every amount. In person: it depends on your ticket size — under $100 Stripe wins, over $100 Square wins, at $100 it's a tie.

Why is Square's online rate so high now?

Square raised it from 2.9% to 3.3% in its October 2025 pricing overhaul. That's what flipped online decisively toward Stripe.

Can I use both?

Many sellers do — Square for the counter, Stripe for the website. Nothing stops you from picking the cheaper rate per channel.

Rates: Square Free plan and Stripe US, verified October 2026. Processors change pricing periodically — verify current rates before deciding.
