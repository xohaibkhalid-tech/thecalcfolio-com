Watch out for outdated Mercari calculators

Mercari changed its fees in 2025. Before that, sellers paid 10% plus a 2.9% + $0.50 processing fee. A lot of calculators and blog posts still use the old numbers, or a $1 minimum fee that isn't on Mercari's current fee page. If a tool shows a fee well above 10% of your item and shipping total, it's probably out of date.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Mercari take from a sale?
10% of the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays, for listings created or updated since January 6, 2025. On a $50 item with $8 buyer-paid shipping, the fee is $5.80 and you keep $44.20 from the item price.
Does Mercari still charge a payment processing fee?
Not to sellers on new or updated listings. Mercari's help center says there is no separate payment processing fee on those listings; buyers instead pay a Buyer Protection fee of 3.6%.
Why is the Mercari fee charged on shipping too?
Mercari calculates its 10% on the total the buyer pays for the item and shipping. If you offer free shipping by building it into the price, the fee works out the same, so price based on the total, not just the item.
How much does Mercari Instant Pay cost?
$3 per cash-out to an eligible debit card, once a day and up to $600 a month. Direct deposit to your bank is free.
Is there a minimum Mercari fee?
Mercari's fee page doesn't list a minimum, so a $10 sale with no buyer-paid shipping costs $1. Some calculators online add a $1 minimum or the old 2.9% + $0.50 processing fee; check the date before trusting them.
