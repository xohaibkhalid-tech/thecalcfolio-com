Goods & Services vs Friends & Family vs Checkout

PayPal has three ways people pay each other, and they're priced very differently:

Goods & Services is the cheapest protected option for one-off sales: on $100 it costs $2.99 against $3.98 through Checkout. Checkout makes sense when the payment happens on your own site. For every PayPal rate, including Checkout, cards and micropayments, use the main PayPal fee calculator.

When the 2.99% is worth paying

That 2.99% buys protection. With Goods & Services, a buyer can open a dispute if an item never arrives, and a seller who ships to the address on the transaction with tracking can qualify for Seller Protection. Friends & Family has neither, so a stranger asking you to "send as friends to save the fee" is a classic warning sign.

If the 2.99% feels heavy, price it in. On a $100 sale, asking $103.08 covers it exactly. More ideas in how to reduce PayPal fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the PayPal Goods and Services fee?
For US sellers it's 2.99% of the payment, with no fixed fee, according to PayPal's US fee schedule (last updated October 1, 2026). On $100 you pay $2.99 and keep $97.01. If the buyer is outside the US, PayPal adds another 1.50%.
Who pays the Goods and Services fee, the buyer or the seller?
The seller, meaning whoever receives the money. A US buyer sending a Goods & Services payment from their PayPal balance, bank or card usually pays nothing extra. If you want the buyer to cover it, raise your price using the reverse calculator above.
Is there a fixed fee on PayPal Goods and Services?
Not on US Goods & Services payments. PayPal's US merchant fee schedule lists the rate as 2.99% with no fixed fee, which is why it's cheaper than PayPal Checkout (3.49% + $0.49) on every sale size. Some calculators online still add $0.49; PayPal's own schedule doesn't.
Does PayPal refund the Goods and Services fee if I refund the buyer?
No. Issuing a refund is free, but PayPal keeps the fee it charged when you received the payment. Refund a $200 G&S payment in full and you're still out $5.98.
Is it okay to use Friends & Family to avoid the fee?
Not for sales. Friends & Family is for personal payments, so using it to sell breaks PayPal's terms, and neither side gets Purchase Protection or Seller Protection. If a buyer later disputes, you have very little recourse.
