Square fees — quick answers

Did Square raise its fees?

Yes. In October 2025 Square overhauled pricing: the in-person fixed fee went from $0.10 to $0.15, and online payments on the Free plan jumped from 2.9% + $0.30 to 3.3% + $0.30. Many older guides still show the pre-2025 rates — this calculator uses current ones.

Square vs Stripe — which is cheaper?

It depends where you sell. In-person: Square (2.6% + $0.15) vs Stripe (2.7% + $0.05) — close, Stripe slightly cheaper on small tickets. Online: Stripe (2.9% + $0.30) clearly beats Square's 3.3% + $0.30. Run both our calculators with your real numbers.

Does Square charge a monthly fee?

The Free plan has no monthly fee — you only pay per transaction. The Plus plan costs $49/month and lowers your rates (2.5% + $0.15 in-person, 2.9% + $0.30 online). It only pays off above a certain monthly volume.

What about Afterpay and Cash App Pay?

Afterpay transactions cost 6% + $0.30 across all plans — the most expensive way to get paid on Square. Factor it in before offering buy-now-pay-later.

Is there a fee for cash payments?

No — Square charges no processing fee on cash or check payments recorded in the app. Only card and digital payments incur fees.
