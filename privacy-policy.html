Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What we collect

Almost nothing. Our calculators run entirely in your browser — the numbers you type never leave your device and are never sent to our servers. We don't ask for your name, email, or account.

Cookies & analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages and calculators people use. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects standard usage data — pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and which links to other websites are clicked. It does not receive the numbers you type into our calculators.

You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser. Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Third parties

If we display ads (for example through Google AdSense), Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings. We never sell personal data — we barely collect any.

Questions about this policy: contact@thecalcfolio.com.
