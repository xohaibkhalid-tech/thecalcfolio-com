Frequently asked questions

What are the three fees on every Etsy sale?
Every US sale pays three core fees: a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing. On a $50 sale with no ads that's about $5 — roughly 10%.
Why does Etsy charge the transaction fee on shipping?
Etsy's 6.5% applies to the total sale — item price plus the shipping you charge (and gift wrap). Like eBay, this stops sellers from hiding the price in shipping. Build it into your pricing with the reverse calculator above.
What are Offsite Ads — and can I opt out?
When Etsy advertises your listing on Google, Facebook or elsewhere and it leads to a sale, Etsy takes 15% of that order (shops under $10K/year, can opt out) or 12% (shops over $10K/year — mandatory, no opt-out). It's capped at $100 per order. If you're under $10K and your margins are thin, opting out is usually the smart move.
Do I pay the $0.20 listing fee if my item doesn't sell?
Yes. The $0.20 is charged when you publish, when a listing auto-renews every 4 months, and per unit sold on multi-quantity listings. It's small per listing, but with hundreds of listings the renewals add up — audit dead listings regularly.
How can I reduce my Etsy fees?
Opt out of Offsite Ads while you're eligible, price your items with the full fee stack baked in (use the reverse calculator), avoid currency conversion by matching your listing and payout currencies, and keep listings lean so renewal fees don't pile up.
