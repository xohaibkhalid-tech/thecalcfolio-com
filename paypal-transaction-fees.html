Fees for selling and getting paid

If money comes to you for something you sold, PayPal treats it as a commercial transaction and charges the person receiving it. The rate depends on how the buyer paid you.

PayPal Checkout (3.49% + $0.49) is the button on a website or online store. Goods & Services (2.99%) is when someone pays you directly through the PayPal app for an item, common for marketplace and social media sales. Card payments processed through PayPal cost 2.99% + $0.49, or 2.89% + $0.49 on the advanced integration.

The fixed $0.49 matters most on small sales. On a $10 Checkout sale it's almost five percentage points of extra cost, which is why sellers of low-priced items look at micropayments pricing.

Fees for sending money

Paying a friend or family member in the US is free when the money comes from your PayPal balance or a linked bank account. Use a credit or debit card and the sender pays 2.90% plus a fixed fee. Sending abroad adds 5% of the amount, with a minimum of $0.99 and a maximum of $4.99, plus a conversion spread if the recipient gets a different currency.

Buying something with PayPal as a customer is normally free for the buyer; the seller covers the fee.

Fees for moving money to your bank

A standard transfer to your bank costs nothing and usually arrives in one to a few business days. An Instant Transfer arrives in minutes and costs 1.75%, never less than $0.25 and never more than $25.

The $25 cap kicks in at about $1,428.57, so instant transfers get relatively cheaper on large amounts. On $1,000 the fee is $17.50; on $5,000 it's still $25.

The full cost of a $100 sale

Fees stack, so it's worth seeing a whole sale from start to finish. Say a US buyer pays $100 through PayPal Checkout and you want the money in your bank today:

Step 1, transaction fee: $100 × 3.49% + $0.49 = $3.98. Balance: $96.02.

Step 2, Instant Transfer: $96.02 × 1.75% = $1.68. You receive $94.34.

Total PayPal cost: $5.66, or 5.66% of the sale. Wait for a standard transfer and it's $3.98. Had the buyer paid by Goods & Services instead, it would have been $2.99 before the transfer.

Currency conversion and disputes

When PayPal converts currency for you, it adds a spread over its base exchange rate: 4% when you pay for something in a different currency than it's listed in, or send money so the recipient gets another currency, and 3% for other conversions. Selling to a buyer abroad who pays in euros can therefore cost the 1.50% international fee and a conversion spread.

If a buyer disputes a card payment with their bank, PayPal charges the seller a $20 chargeback fee. Disputes handled inside PayPal cost $15, or $30 for high-volume sellers. Refunds themselves are free, but the original transaction fee isn't returned.

Quick answers

How much does PayPal charge per transaction?

It depends on the type. Selling through PayPal Checkout costs 3.49% + $0.49; a Goods & Services payment costs 2.99% with no fixed fee; a personal payment from your balance or bank is free. Sales to buyers outside the US add 1.50%.

Does PayPal charge to transfer money to my bank?

Not for a standard transfer, which is free and usually takes one to a few business days. An Instant Transfer costs 1.75% of the amount, with a $0.25 minimum and a $25 maximum.

How much does PayPal take from $1,000?

As a PayPal Checkout sale, $35.39, leaving $964.61. As a Goods & Services payment, $29.90, leaving $970.10. Move it to your bank instantly and add about $17 more; a standard transfer is free.

Does the buyer pay PayPal fees?

Usually not. On domestic purchases the seller pays the transaction fee. Buyers only pay when they send a personal payment by card (2.90% plus a fixed fee), send internationally, or pay in a currency that needs converting.

General information only. PayPal changes its fees from time to time; check PayPal's own fee pages for your account before relying on these figures.
