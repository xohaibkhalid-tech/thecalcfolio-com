Grailed fees — quick answers

What's the $120 cliff?

Sell at $119 and you pay 6% commission ($7.14) — you keep $107.22. Sell at $120 and the commission jumps to 9% ($10.80) — you keep $104.52. That single dollar of price costs you $2.70. If your item is near $120, price at $119.

When did Grailed change its fees?

May 20, 2026: commission dropped from a flat 9% to 6% (minimum $1.99) on sales under $120. Sales of $120+ still pay 9%. Many older guides still show the flat 9% — this calculator uses the current tiers.

Does the commission apply to shipping?

If you use Grailed's integrated labels, only the listing price counts toward the $120 tier. If you charge shipping separately, the sale price includes shipping — a $110 item with $15 shipping counts as $125 and lands in the 9% tier.

Are there listing fees?

No — no listing fees, no subscriptions. You only pay when something sells: commission + payment processing.

Grailed vs eBay for streetwear?

On a $100 sale: Grailed takes ~$9.98 total (6% + processing); eBay takes ~$14.00. Grailed is cheaper and reaches a focused streetwear audience — but eBay's buyer pool is far larger.
