PayPal vs Stripe Fees: The Honest Comparison

Stripe wins on price, every time: 2.9% + $0.30 vs PayPal's 2.99% + $0.49 for card payments. On a $100 sale that's $3.20 vs $3.48 — Stripe keeps $0.28 more in your pocket. PayPal's edge isn't price; it's the 400+ million people who already have an account and check out faster with it.

No tie point, no crossover — Stripe is cheaper at every amount for standard card processing. The gap is small per sale and real at volume: 1,000 sales at $100 = $280 saved.

Where PayPal fights back

  • Checkout conversion: hundreds of millions of buyers have PayPal and pay in two clicks. A cheaper processor that converts worse can cost you more than it saves.
  • Micropayments: PayPal's 4.99% + $0.09 micropayment rate beats its own card rate (2.99% + $0.49) on sales under $20 — Stripe has no equivalent.
  • No code needed: PayPal buttons work without a developer. Stripe needs integration (or a platform that includes it).
  • Buyer trust: for unknown stores, the PayPal badge still reassures nervous first-time buyers.

Tip: Many stores run both: Stripe as the default card processor, PayPal as the express option. You pay a bit more on PayPal transactions and (often) convert better overall.

The verdict

On fees alone: Stripe. It's not close at any transaction size.

On business reality: it depends. If your customers are PayPal people (eBay-style buyers, international, older demographics), dropping PayPal to save $0.28 a sale is penny-wise. If you're a SaaS or a modern DTC brand with developers on hand, Stripe is the obvious pick.

The real answer for most growing stores: both, with Stripe default.

Quick answers

Is Stripe really cheaper than PayPal?

For card processing, yes — 2.9% + $0.30 vs 2.99% + $0.49, cheaper at every transaction size. PayPal Checkout (3.49% + $0.49) is wider still.

Why do so many stores still use PayPal?

Conversion. Familiar checkout, buyer protection reputation, and hundreds of millions of funded accounts. The fee premium buys trust and speed for many customer bases.

Can I use both?

Yes — most platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce) support Stripe for cards plus PayPal Express side by side. Common setup, no conflict.
