eBay Seller Fees Explained

Calculate it for your own numbersFree, no sign-up — every fee on one receipt.
eBay Fee Calculator →

eBay takes 13.6% of the total sale (item price + shipping + tax) plus a $0.40 per-order fee on orders over $10 ($0.30 on orders of $10 or less) for most categories. On a $100 sale, that's $14.00 in fees before any extras like promoted listings.

The fee stack

FeeAmount
Final value fee (most categories)13.6% of total
Per-order fee$0.40 on orders over $10 · $0.30 on orders of $10 or less
Insertion feesFirst 250/month free, then $0.35 each
Promoted listings (optional)You set the ad rate, e.g. 5–10%
International fee1.65% on international sales

The 13.6% applies to the total the buyer pays — including shipping and sales tax. Free shipping doesn't dodge it; the fee just comes out of your item price instead.

Real examples

  • $25 sale: $3.40 + $0.40 = $3.80 (15.2% effective)
  • $100 sale: $13.60 + $0.40 = $14.00 (14.0% effective)
  • $500 sale: $68.00 + $0.40 = $68.40 (13.7% effective)

Notice the pattern: the flat per-order fee stings small sellers most. On a $10 sale the $0.30 fee is 3% all by itself — one reason low-dollar eBay selling is brutally thin.

Tip: The per-order fee is charged even on $1 sales. Bundle small items into lots instead of listing them individually — one $0.40 fee beats ten $0.30 fees.

Fees you can actually reduce

  • Earn Top Rated Plus status: can cut the final value fee by 10% in many categories.
  • Skip promoted listings until a listing proves it converts organically.
  • Stay under 250 listings/month unless the extra insertion fees pay for themselves.
  • Price shipping accurately — the fee hits shipping too, so undercharging shipping costs you twice.

Quick answers

How much does eBay take per sale?

13.6% of the total (price + shipping + tax) plus $0.40 per order on orders over $10 ($0.30 on orders of $10 or less) for most categories.

Does eBay charge fees on shipping?

Yes — the final value fee applies to the total amount the buyer pays, including shipping and sales tax.

Are eBay fees negotiable?

Not directly, but top-rated seller status effectively discounts them, and high-volume sellers can sometimes negotiate custom rates with eBay.
