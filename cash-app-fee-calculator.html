Cash App instant transfer fee, explained

Cash App no longer charges everyone the same instant fee. Your rate sits somewhere between 0.5% and 2.5%, with a minimum of $0.25 to $1 and a cap of $75. The app shows your exact fee on the screen before you confirm the transfer — type that percentage into the calculator above and it will match.

The easy way to pay $0: choose the standard transfer. It's free and lands in 1–3 business days. Instant only buys speed.

Cash App fees — quick answers

How much does Cash App charge to cash out instantly?

Instant transfers cost 0.5% to 2.5% of the amount, with a minimum fee of $0.25 to $1 and a maximum of $75. Cash App sets your exact rate and shows it before you confirm. Standard transfers that take 1–3 business days are free.

What is the Cash App fee for $100?

Sending $100 to a friend from your balance, bank or debit card is free. Cashing out $100 instantly costs $0.50 to $2.50 depending on your rate. Receiving $100 into a Business account costs $2.75 (2.6% + $0.15).

What is the Cash App business fee?

Cash App charges 2.6% + $0.15 on each payment a customer sends to your Cash App Business account, and 3% on Tap to Pay payments. The fee is taken from the payment before it reaches your balance.

Does Cash App charge for using a credit card?

Yes. Sending money with a linked credit card costs the sender 3% of the amount. Using a debit card, your bank account or your Cash App balance is free.

How do I avoid Cash App fees?

Use standard transfers instead of instant, fund payments from your balance, bank or debit card instead of a credit card, and use a personal account only for friends and family. For regular sales, compare business rates on other apps first.
