eBay vs Etsy Fees: Which One Takes More of Your Sale?

Short answer: Etsy is usually cheaper per sale — until it isn't. The full answer depends on your price point and one Etsy fee that blindsides new sellers. Let's do the actual math instead of guessing.

The fees, side by side (2026)

FeeeBayEtsy
Final value / transaction fee13.6% (most categories)6.5%
Payment processingincluded above3% + $0.25
Per-order / listing fee$0.30–$0.40 per order$0.20 per listing
Offsite ads—12–15% if a sale comes from ads

Etsy's headline rate looks dramatically lower. But Etsy's fees stack — transaction fee plus payment processing plus listing fee — while eBay's 13.6% is mostly all-inclusive. That's the part people miss.

Worked example: a $50 sale

eBayEtsy
Percentage fees$50 × 13.6% = $6.80$50 × 9.5% = $4.75
Fixed fees+ $0.40+ $0.45
Total fee$7.20$5.20
You keep$42.80$44.80

Verdict on a $50 sale: Etsy wins by $2.00. On handmade and vintage goods in the $20–$100 range, Etsy is consistently the cheaper platform — as long as offsite ads stay out of the picture.

The offsite ads trap: if Etsy shows your product on Google and it sells, Etsy takes 15% extra (12% once you pass $10K/year in sales) — and you can't opt out until you hit that $10K. One ad-driven sale can wipe out the fee advantage of ten normal ones. Check your Payment account page regularly to see which sales came from ads.

So which should you sell on?

Honestly? Fees are only half the decision:

  • Sell on Etsy if your products are handmade, vintage, or craft supplies. The audience is pre-filtered for exactly that, and the fees are lower per sale.
  • Sell on eBay if you sell used goods, electronics, collectibles, or anything that needs an auction format. eBay's buyer pool is far bigger — a lower fee on zero sales is worth nothing.
  • Sell on both if your products fit both audiences. Plenty of sellers do. Just run the numbers per item instead of assuming one platform always wins.

One more thing people forget: your time is a fee too. Etsy's listing and SEO game rewards constant optimization; eBay rewards volume. Pick the one whose grind you actually enjoy.

Quick answers

Is Etsy's 6.5% fee the whole story?

No, and this trips up beginners constantly. Your real Etsy cost per sale is 6.5% transaction + 3% payment processing + $0.25 + the $0.20 listing fee — roughly 9.5% + $0.45 before ads. Still usually beats eBay, but it's not "6.5%."

Can I avoid Etsy's offsite ads fee?

Not until your shop passes $10,000 in sales in a 12-month period. Before that, enrollment is mandatory. After that you can opt out (and the fee drops to 12% if you stay in). Yes, sellers complain about this constantly. No, Etsy hasn't changed it.

Does eBay charge the fee on shipping too?

Yes — eBay's final value fee applies to the total including shipping and tax. So "free shipping" isn't free; build it into your price. Our eBay calculator handles this automatically.
