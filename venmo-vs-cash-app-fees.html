Venmo vs Cash App Fees: Which Costs You Less?

Calculate it for your own numbersFree, no sign-up — every fee on one receipt.

The short answer: for business payments, Venmo wins at every amount — 1.9% + $0.10 beats Cash App's 2.6% + $0.15 because both the percentage and the fixed fee are lower. For personal transfers between friends, both are free.

Two apps, two fee philosophies. Here's the breakdown.

There's no crossover point any more: Cash App's business fee rose from a flat 2.75% to 2.6% + $0.15 per payment, so Venmo is cheaper on every amount — about 12 cents cheaper on a $10 sale and $3.55 cheaper on $500. In-person Tap to Pay costs 3% on Cash App and 2.29% + $0.09 on Venmo.

Cashing out: the speed tax

Both apps move money to your bank free if you wait 1–3 days. Need it now?

  • Venmo instant transfer: 1.75% (min $0.25, max $25)
  • Cash App instant deposit: 0.5%–2.5% (your rate varies; capped at $75)

Cash App's range is wider and less predictable — your exact rate is set by the app. Venmo's flat 1.75% is at least honest about what it'll cost.

Free money move: standard transfers on both apps cost $0. The instant fee is purely a patience tax. Unless a bill is due today, wait the 1–3 days.

Beyond fees: what else differs

  • Audience: Venmo has the larger US user base — more customers already have it. Cash App skews younger and pairs with its debit card and stock/bitcoin features.
  • UK availability: Cash App operates in the UK too; Venmo is US-only.
  • Seller protection: both are thin compared to PayPal or Stripe. Neither is ideal as your only business payment method for high-value sales.

Quick answers

Is Cash App free for personal use?

Yes — sending and receiving between friends is free on both apps when funded by bank or debit. Fees only appear for business receiving, credit-card funding (3% on both), and instant cash-outs.

Why is Cash App's instant fee a range?

Cash App sets your instant deposit rate individually (0.5%–2.5%) — the app shows your exact fee before you confirm. If yours is at the high end, Venmo's flat 1.75% may actually be cheaper for speed.

Can I have business profiles on both?

Yes, and many freelancers do — accept whichever the client prefers, and price knowing each app's cut. The fee difference only matters at volume.
